Our Calling

Where Faith Becomes Action

Kingdom Connections is a Christ-centered community dedicated to bringing together individuals, families, businesses, churches, and ministries for the purpose of advancing God's Kingdom. We believe that lasting transformation happens through meaningful relationships, servant leadership, prayer, and practical support that help people thrive spiritually, personally, and professionally.

Our mission is to connect people with Kingdom-minded resources, opportunities, and relationships that strengthen communities, encourage collaboration, and move people closer to Jesus Christ.

At Kingdom Connections, we believe that God has called every believer to influence their sphere of life for His glory. By building bridges between the Church, the marketplace, and the community, we seek to create an environment where people can support one another, share resources, develop trusted relationships, and work together to make a lasting difference.

Our Story

A Vision Born in Faith

Carried forward in trust — from a divine calling to a global movement.

2008

The Vision

Dr. Cornell Haan received a vision from God: a day when Christians around the world would be connected through their smartphones to pray, encourage one another, share resources, and advance God's Kingdom together. He faithfully embraced the mission God had placed on his heart.

2019

Carried Forward

Dr. Cornell shared that vision with his co-partners, Thomas Ryan and Brian Taylor, who continued to carry it forward even after Dr. Cornell went home to be with the Lord. They trusted that God would bring together the right people, technology, and opportunity.

2026

The Launch

After years of prayer, preparation, and anticipation, God brought that team together. The result was the launch of the Kingdom Connections website and the Praying Planet App — tools to connect believers, churches, ministries, prayer groups, and businesses across the globe.

Today

A Growing Movement

Kingdom Connections exists to unite Christians around prayer, encouragement, discipleship, service, and Kingdom impact. We are excited to see thousands — and ultimately millions — of believers connected to share the hope of Jesus Christ with the world.

Founder

About Dr. Cornell Haan

A day when Christians around the world would be connected to pray, encourage one another, share resources, and advance God's Kingdom together.

Dr. Cornell Haan, affectionately referred to as "Corkie", devoted his life to serving God and equipping believers for prayer, revival, and Kingdom impact. He pastored five churches and founded, funded, or supported numerous ministries and national initiatives.

His Accomplishments Include

  • Founder of Kingdom Connections
  • Co-Founder of the Presidential Prayer Team
  • Founding partner with Edwin Louis Cole in the early development of Promise Keepers
  • Evangelical point person of Mel Gibson's film The Passion of the Christ
  • Contributor to Congressional Prayer Teams and Congressional Prayer District TV outreach
  • National Facilitator for Mission America Coalition
  • Founder, Publisher, and Editor-in-Chief of Prayer Connect Magazine
  • Editor of Pray! Magazine
  • Leader and supporter of national movements including See You at the Pole, March for Jesus, Jesus Day, and Acquire the Fire

Dr. Cornell's legacy lives on through Kingdom Connections and the countless lives that continue to be impacted by his vision of a connected, praying, and united Body of Christ.
