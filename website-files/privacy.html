Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kingdom Connections (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide (such as name, email, and membership details) and technical information needed to operate the site (such as browser type and approximate location).

Analytics & Cookies

We use optional analytics cookies only after you give explicit consent through our cookie banner. You may Accept Analytics, Reject Analytics, or Customize preferences. You can change your choice at any time via Cookie Settings in the footer. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we treat analytics as opt-out by default until you choose otherwise.

We do not load third-party analytics scripts, pixels, or tracking tools until you opt in. Consent is stored in a first-party cookie named analytics_consent for up to 12 months.

How We Use Information

To provide membership services, respond to inquiries, improve the site, and communicate about Kingdom Connections programs — never to sell your personal data.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at info@kingdomconnections.world.
