About Steven G. Sandoval

Steve has been an entrepreneur, educator, mentor, and coach for more than 40 years.

At age 18, after becoming a born-again Christian and graduating from high school, Steve joined the USAF. After two years of military tech school and two years of college pursuing a degree to teach history and coach basketball and golf, Steve also started to pursue business ventures with fellow church members and was mentored by entrepreneurs in this group. This eventually led him to become a lifelong, passionate student of business and politics and a full-time entrepreneur, investing in businesses and helping others launch, market, and promote their new business ventures.

Steve has been on a special and unique journey which has led him to become a bold and vocal modern-day Patriot, a Christian Soldier, and a Champion for Free Enterprise. Steve is now called upon to “sound the alarm” to wake up Americans and teach and train the next generation of freedom-loving leaders, champions, and patriots through Freedom Quest Academy. His passion and mission are to encourage people to dream and work in pursuing their own dreams and connect to their God-given purpose. Steve teaches that we are called to thrive and soar like eagles and not struggle and live like “chickens and turkeys.” Steve works to nexus-connect God’s kingdom servant leaders to unite and partner together using Bible-based education and helping connect the best of the best. Steve founded SGS Promotions & Marketing Company in 2010 to help expand the current membership of business owners and community members across America.

Steve’s mission is to unite American Patriots as well as Christian and Veteran small business owners across the country to take back America to God, the Constitution, and “We the People.” Steve is a public speaker and is currently pursuing exposure in radio and video production. Steve is married to Annie, and they live in Phoenix, AZ, working together in business and ministry, SGS Ministries, teaching, preaching, and Sharing God’s Solutions. Together they have a blended family of seven grown children and four beautiful grandchildren.
