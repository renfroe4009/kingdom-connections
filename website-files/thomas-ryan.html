About Thomas Ryan

Thomas Ryan is a seasoned executive leader, educator, and ministry innovator with a career spanning corporate finance, higher education, and nonprofit development. He holds an MBA from Indiana University, a BBA from Ohio University, and a Doctor of Ministry from the Phoenix University of Theology.

His professional background includes serving as a Certified Public Accountant with Ernst & Young, a Chief Financial Officer for multiple organizations, and a business consultant specializing in organizational turnarounds. He is known for implementing strategic planning, accounting systems, and management frameworks that restore stability and drive growth.

For more than three decades, Mr. Ryan has shaped students and professionals through higher education. He has served on the Grand Canyon University faculty since 2014, taught at the University of Phoenix for over 30 years, and at Regis University for more than 15 years.

In the nonprofit sector, he has been deeply involved in founding and strengthening faith-based organizations. His leadership includes cofounding Life Transitions Academy, Life Skills Institute, the Arizona Alliance for Faith-Based Action, Abounding Service, and the Recovery Peer Support Network. He currently serves on the board of a nonprofit ministry.

Today, he is CEO of Kingdom Connections, a movement designed to unite Christians in prayer while connecting believers with products and services offered by Christian-owned businesses and espousing the seven mountains of influence in winning nations for Christ.
