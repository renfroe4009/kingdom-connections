About Brian Taylor

Brian Taylor, born in Toronto, Canada, is a motivational leader dedicated to teaching and educating in unique and unconventional ways. He holds a BA in Psychology from McMaster University, a BPE from McMaster University, and an MA in Education from the University of Toronto. He is also a licensed minister through Independent Assemblies of God International.

For over 30 years, Brian has taught, coached, and impacted the lives of elementary students as well as teachers in Ontario, Canada. With a special gift to recognize the potential in students, he has inspired and encouraged many individuals to excel in learning beyond their own expectations.

Now residing in Arizona, Brian has continued his work in education for over two decades, teaching in elementary schools while also leading discipleship classes in his church and his home. In addition, he devoted 10 years to teaching through jail ministry, helping individuals discover hope, purpose, and spiritual growth.

Currently, Brian serves as the COO of Kingdom Connections, a global movement focused on uniting Christians in prayer, connecting believers with products and services offered by Christian-owned businesses, and encouraging believers to take action within the seven areas of cultural influence, to help win nations for Christ.
