The 7 Mountains of Influence

Where will you make an impact? God has called every believer to influence their sphere of life for His glory.

A Core Calling

Influence and Transform Culture for Christ

The 7 Mountains of Influence are the primary places where culture is shaped: family, the Church, education, government, business, arts, and media. Kingdom Connections exists to help believers pray, connect, and take their place in the mountain God has called them to.

At Kingdom Connections, we believe God has called every believer to influence their sphere of life for His glory. By building bridges between the Church, the marketplace, and the community, we create a place where people can support one another, share resources, and work together to make a lasting difference.

These mountains are not competing kingdoms. They are seven arenas of daily life where faith becomes action — in the home, in the sanctuary, in the classroom, in public service, in the marketplace, in the studio, and in the public conversation.

Each mountain is modeled by a Bible hero whose life still teaches us how to lead with faith, integrity, and courage.

Mountain 01

Family

The Foundation of Society · Home, Legacy, Relationships

The family is God's first institution and the cornerstone of every healthy civilization. It is within the family that children first learn love, faith, character, responsibility, and biblical values. Strong marriages and healthy families create stable communities and prepare future generations to lead with wisdom and integrity.

Today, families face tremendous challenges including broken relationships, financial pressures, addiction, loneliness, and confusion regarding identity and purpose. Christians are called to strengthen marriages, nurture children, care for aging parents, mentor younger families, and model Christ-centered homes that reflect God's love.

  • Strengthening marriages
  • Raising children in biblical truth
  • Mentoring young families
  • Supporting single parents and widows
  • Promoting healthy relationships
  • Restoring hope to broken families
God did not begin His covenant with a building, a government, or a business — He began it with a family. The family mountain is where identity is formed, values are taught, and faith is passed from one generation to the next. The home is where faith lives first. Abraham · Father of nations

Joshua 24:15 — “As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.”

The Call and Promise

  • Genesis 12:1–2 — The Lord had said to Abram, “Go from your country, your people and your father’s household to the land I will show you. I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you; I will make your name great, and you will be a blessing.”
  • Genesis 12:3 — “I will bless those who bless you, and whoever curses you I will curse; and all peoples on earth will be blessed through you.”

His Faith and Righteousness

  • Genesis 15:6 — Abram believed the Lord, and he credited it to him as righteousness.

The Test of Faith

  • Genesis 22:2 — Then God said, “Take your son, your only son, whom you love — Isaac — and go to the region of Moriah. Sacrifice him there as a burnt offering on a mountain I will show you.”

Mountain 02

Religion (Church)

Advancing God's Kingdom · Faith & Spiritual Leadership

The Church exists to glorify God, make disciples, proclaim the Gospel, and equip believers to serve Christ in every area of life. The Church is not confined to a building — it is the people of God carrying His presence into their communities.

Beyond worship services, the Church provides spiritual guidance, prayer, biblical teaching, compassion, outreach, counseling, missions, and hope. It serves as the spiritual heartbeat of society and equips believers to impact every other mountain. Kingdom-minded churches work together rather than compete, recognizing that we are one Body serving one King.

  • Sharing the Gospel
  • Making disciples
  • Equipping leaders
  • Prayer and intercession
  • Missions and outreach
  • Unity among believers
True spiritual leadership begins with intimacy with God. Before I stood before Pharaoh, I stood before the burning bush. Spiritual authority does not come from position or title — it comes from obedience and surrender to the Lord. Moses · Deliverer of God's people

Matthew 28:19–20 — “Go therefore and make disciples of all nations…”

  • Exodus 3:14 — “God said to Moses, ‘I AM WHO I AM. This is what you are to say to the Israelites: “I AM has sent me to you.”’”
  • Exodus 33:11 — “The Lord would speak to Moses face to face, as one speaks to a friend.”
  • Hebrews 11:24–25 — “By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be known as the son of Pharaoh’s daughter. He chose to be mistreated along with the people of God rather than to enjoy the fleeting pleasures of sin.”

Mountain 03

Education

Shaping Hearts and Minds · Wisdom, Teaching, Knowledge

Education influences how people think, reason, solve problems, and understand the world around them. Teachers, professors, homeschool parents, administrators, coaches, mentors, and trainers all have tremendous influence over future generations.

Biblical education goes beyond academics — it develops wisdom, integrity, servant leadership, and critical thinking grounded in God's truth. Christians can influence education by teaching excellence, encouraging lifelong learning, mentoring students, and promoting character alongside knowledge.

  • Teaching truth with excellence
  • Mentoring students
  • Encouraging critical thinking
  • Developing future leaders
  • Supporting teachers
  • Promoting biblical values and character
When God offered me anything I desired, I did not ask for riches or power — I asked for wisdom. Education is not simply about information; it is about understanding truth and teaching people how to live wisely. True wisdom begins with God. Solomon · Wisest of kings

Proverbs 1:7 — “The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge.”

  • Asking for Wisdom (1 Kings 3:5–12) — God tells Solomon to ask for anything, and Solomon requests wisdom instead of riches, which pleases God.
  • Solomon’s Wisdom (1 Kings 4:29–34) — Describes the immense wisdom, proverbs, and knowledge God gave him that attracted international fame.
  • Song of Solomon 3:4 — “I have found the one whom my soul loves.”
  • Solomon’s Turn Away (1 Kings 11:4) — As Solomon grew old, his wives turned his heart after other gods, and his heart was not fully devoted to the Lord.

Mountain 04

Government

Promoting Justice and Freedom · Leadership, Justice, Authority

Government exists to establish justice, maintain order, protect freedom, and serve the people. Public officials, judges, law enforcement officers, military personnel, first responders, and civic leaders all play important roles in preserving peace and protecting communities.

Christians are called to pray for leaders, participate responsibly in civic life, vote wisely, advocate for righteousness, defend the vulnerable, and serve with integrity wherever God places them. Leadership is ultimately about serving others with humility, wisdom, and accountability.

  • Public service
  • Law enforcement
  • Public policy
  • Justice and mercy
  • Protecting religious liberty
  • Civic engagement
I served in the courts of kings that did not always honor the God of heaven, yet I remained faithful. True authority is not rooted in pride or control; it is rooted in accountability to God and service to others. Even in positions of influence, integrity must never be compromised. Daniel · Faithful in the palace

Proverbs 29:2 — “When the righteous thrive, the people rejoice.”

  • Setting — The 6th century BC in Babylon, after Jerusalem and its temple were attacked and plundered.
  • Stories (Chapters 1–6) — Accounts of Daniel and his friends (Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego) remaining faithful to God under foreign rulers, including surviving the fiery furnace and the lion’s den.
  • Visions (Chapters 7–12) — Apocalyptic visions regarding future kingdoms, world empires, and the ultimate triumph of God’s eternal kingdom.
  • The primary story of Joseph in the Bible is found in the Book of Genesis, spanning chapters 37 through 50. It tells the account of Jacob’s son, who was sold into slavery by his brothers, rose to become a ruler in Egypt, and ultimately saved his family from starvation.

Mountain 05

Business

Stewardship, Innovation, and Generosity · Economics, Provision

Business is one of God's primary means of providing for families, creating opportunity, solving problems, and funding Kingdom work. Entrepreneurs, executives, employees, tradespeople, investors, and business owners all have opportunities to honor God through ethical leadership and faithful stewardship.

Business done with integrity creates jobs, serves customers, strengthens communities, and generates resources that support ministries, missions, and charitable work. Success becomes most meaningful when it becomes a blessing to others.

  • Creating jobs
  • Serving customers with excellence
  • Ethical leadership
  • Financial stewardship
  • Generosity
  • Supporting Kingdom initiatives
Business is more than profit. It is stewardship — the wise management of resources so that people, families, and nations can flourish. Wealth is not meant to control people; it is meant to serve people and honor God. Every resource ultimately belongs to the Lord. Joseph · Steward in famine and plenty

Colossians 3:23 — “Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.”

  • The Coat and the Dreams (Genesis 37) — Jacob loves Joseph most and gives him a special coat. Joseph has dreams that his family will bow down to him. His brothers grow jealous, sell him to traders for 20 pieces of silver, and tell their father he is dead.
  • Potiphar and Prison (Genesis 39–40) — Joseph works in Egypt for Potiphar. He stays loyal to God, resists Potiphar’s wife, and goes to jail after a false charge. He correctly explains dreams for other prisoners.
  • Ruler of Egypt (Genesis 41–42) — Pharaoh has a troubling dream. Joseph explains it: Egypt will have seven good years then seven bad years of hunger. Pharaoh makes Joseph the second-in-command to save food.
  • Forgiveness and Reunion (Genesis 45–50) — Severe hunger brings Joseph’s brothers to Egypt for food. Joseph forgives them, saying God turned their bad actions into good to save many lives.

Mountain 06

Arts & Entertainment

Inspiring Culture Through Creativity · Expression, Worship, Culture

Music, visual arts, literature, film, television, theater, sports, and digital media profoundly influence the values, emotions, and imagination of society. God is the ultimate Creator, and He has gifted people with creativity to reflect His beauty, truth, and goodness.

Christian artists, musicians, writers, filmmakers, athletes, designers, and performers have unique opportunities to inspire hope, communicate truth, and point others toward Christ through excellence and creativity.

  • Music and worship
  • Film and television
  • Literature and publishing
  • Fine arts
  • Sports
  • Digital content creation
Before I wore a crown, I carried a harp. Through music, poetry, and song I learned that creativity is a gift from God meant to touch hearts and shape culture. When I played before King Saul, peace entered the room. True creativity flows from the Creator Himself. David · Shepherd, psalmist, king

Exodus 35:35 — “He has filled them with skill to do every sort of work…”

  • Anointing by Samuel: 1 Samuel 16:1–13 — Young David is chosen by God and anointed while tending sheep.
  • David and Goliath: 1 Samuel 17:41–51 — The famous battle where David defeated the Philistine giant with a sling and stone.
  • The Covenant with David: 2 Samuel 7:8–16 — God promises David that his dynasty and throne will be established forever.
  • Sin and Repentance: 2 Samuel 11–12 — David’s adultery with Bathsheba, the murder of Uriah, and his repentance after being confronted by the prophet Nathan.
  • Psalms of David: Psalm 23 and Psalm 51 — Reflect his deep relationship with God, praise, and plea for mercy.
  • A Heart for God: Acts 13:22 highlights why God chose David to fulfill His will.
  • Messianic Lineage: Matthew 1:1 and Isaiah 11:1 trace Jesus Christ’s physical ancestry back to King David, fulfilling the eternal throne promise.

Mountain 07

Media & Communications

Influencing Hearts Through Information · Messaging, Influence

Media shapes public opinion more rapidly than almost any other mountain. News organizations, journalists, podcasters, broadcasters, social media creators, publishers, bloggers, marketers, and communicators influence how people understand current events, form opinions, and connect with one another.

Today's digital world offers unprecedented opportunities to spread truth, encourage others, educate communities, and share the Gospel across the globe. Christians are called to communicate with honesty, wisdom, compassion, and excellence while using every available platform to shine the light of Christ.

  • Journalism
  • Social media
  • Broadcasting and podcasting
  • Publishing
  • Marketing
  • Storytelling
In my day I traveled cities, spoke in marketplaces, and wrote letters that carried truth across nations. Today, media carries messages farther and faster than ever before. Words can divide or unite, spread fear or bring hope. God calls communicators to speak with wisdom, integrity, and courage. Paul the Apostle · Messenger of the Gospel

Philippians 4:8 — “Whatever is true… think about these things.”

  • The Conversion: Acts 9:1–19 — Saul, who once hurt believers, meets Jesus on the road to Damascus and changes his life.
  • The First Missionary Journey: Acts 13:1–3 — Paul is sent out by the Holy Spirit to share the good news.
  • The Philippian Jailer: Acts 16:25–34 — Paul sings in prison, an earthquake happens, and a jailer learns about Jesus.
  • Salvation by Grace: Ephesians 2:8–9 — We are saved by God’s grace through faith, not by our own good works.
  • New Life in Christ: 2 Corinthians 5:17 — Anyone who belongs to Christ is a new person.
  • The Power of Contentment: Philippians 4:12–13 — Paul says he can face anything through Christ who gives him strength.
